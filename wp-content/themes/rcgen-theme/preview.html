Our Four Organisations

Each program has a distinct identity, serving a specific need in the Vrygrond community — united by the same heart and mission.

🕊️ Faith & Outreach

RCGEN Ministry

Spirit-led community church and outreach based in Vrygrond. We proclaim the Gospel, disciple believers, and serve those on the margins through faith-based programs and prayer.

  • Weekly worship services
  • Community prayer & discipleship
  • Youth & family outreach
Learn more
🤝 Community Welfare

NGN Help

Providing humanitarian aid, elderly support, and crisis intervention to families and individuals in Vrygrond. No one should face hardship alone.

  • Emergency food & clothing aid
  • Elderly care & home visits
  • Crisis counselling & referrals
Learn more
🎓 Early Childhood Education

RCGEN Educare

Quality crèche and ECD centre preparing children ages 0–6 for school and life. We believe every child deserves a strong foundation built on love and learning.

  • Registered crèche for ages 0–6
  • CAPS-aligned ECD curriculum
  • School-readiness & parent support
Learn more
🍽️ Nutrition & Food Security

Feeding Scheme

Serving nutritious, warm meals to children and families in need every week. Because no child in Vrygrond should go to bed hungry.

  • Weekly hot meals distribution
  • Nutrition education for families
  • Food parcel drives & donations
Learn more
