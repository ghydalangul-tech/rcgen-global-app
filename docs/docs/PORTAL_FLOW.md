# RCGEN Global App – 4-in-1 Portal System

RCGEN Global App operates as a unified platform with four separate login portals under one system.

Each portal has independent roles, permissions, and dashboards.

---

## 1️⃣ RCGEN Educare Portal

### Users:
- Principal
- Teachers
- Admin
- Parents

### Core Modules:
- Child Registration
- Attendance Tracking
- Class Management
- Fees & Invoices
- Parent Communication
- Reports & Compliance

### Access Control:
- Parents only see their own child.
- Teachers see assigned classes.
- Principal has full Educare access.

---

## 2️⃣ RCGEN Church Portal

### Users:
- Apostle (Full Access)
- Church Admin
- Ministry Leaders
- Members

### Core Modules:
- Member Database
- Visitor & Follow-Up System
- Prayer & Counseling
- Ministries & Departments
- Tithes & Giving Records
- Events & Calendar
- Sermons & Media

### Access Control:
- Members see their own giving & profile.
- Ministry leaders see only their department.
- Apostle sees everything.

---

## 3️⃣ RCGEN Foundation Portal

### Users:
- Foundation Admin
- Volunteers
- Beneficiary Coordinators

### Core Modules:
- Beneficiary Registration
- Feeding Scheme Tracking
- Donation Records
- Volunteer Scheduling
- Impact Reports

### Access Control:
- Volunteers see assigned tasks only.
- Admin has full Foundation access.

---

## 4️⃣ RCGEN Core / Group Portal

### Users:
- Directors
- Governance Team
- Executive Management

### Core Modules:
- Governance Documents
- Risk Management
- HR & Staff Records
- Financial Overview (All Portals)
- Compliance Tracking
- Strategic Dashboard

### Access Control:
- Restricted to leadership only.
- Central oversight of all portals.

---

## 🔐 Unified Login Architecture

- Single RCGEN App
- User selects portal
- Role-based authentication
- Data separated per portal
- Central admin supervision
